Delivery Information

Shipping Policy

Everything you need to know about our shipping process.

Shipping Coverage

The Fashion currently ships to all serviceable PIN codes across India. We partner with trusted logistics providers to ensure safe and timely delivery of your orders.

Shipping Charges

  • Orders below ₹5,000: Flat shipping charge of ₹199
  • Orders of ₹5,000 and above: Free shipping

Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next business day.

Delivery Timelines

  • Metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad): 3–5 business days
  • Tier 2 & Tier 3 cities: 5–7 business days
  • Remote/rural areas: 7–10 business days

Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a confirmation email and SMS with your tracking number and a link to track your shipment in real time.

Delivery Attempts

Our delivery partners will make up to 3 delivery attempts. If the delivery is unsuccessful after three attempts, the order will be returned to our warehouse and a refund will be initiated.

Damaged in Transit

If your order arrives damaged or tampered with, please refuse the delivery and contact us immediately at support@thefashion.live or +91 98100 54321. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no additional cost.
