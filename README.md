# The Fashion — Luxury Ecommerce Store

A premium fashion and clothing ecommerce website designed with a luxury dark-gold aesthetic, responsive layout, full client-side cart functionality, and compliance pages.

## 🌟 Features
- **Hero Showcase:** Dual-column auto-scrolling garment visual showcase with continuous marquee ribbon.
- **Complete Catalog:** 36 curated fashion items across Tops, Shirts, Knitwear, Outerwear, and Bottoms with real photographic assets.
- **Interactive Shopping Cart:** Slide-out drawer with quantity controls, subtotal calculation, free shipping rule threshold, and `localStorage` persistence.
- **Checkout Simulator:** Multi-step billing details, address capture, and simulated payment gateway selection (Razorpay & Cashfree).
- **Compliance & Legal Suite:** Fully integrated Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Shipping Policy, and Refund Policy.
- **Clean Responsive Architecture:** Mobile-friendly hamburger navigation and tablet/desktop layouts.
