Returns & Exchanges

Refund Policy

Our commitment to your satisfaction.

Return Window

We offer a 7-day return window from the date of delivery. If 7 days have passed since delivery, we are unable to offer a return or refund.

Eligibility for Returns

To be eligible for a return, your item must be:

  • Unused and in the same condition as received
  • In the original packaging with all tags attached
  • Accompanied by the original invoice or order confirmation

Non-Returnable Items

  • Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Non-Returnable"
  • Accessories such as scarves, belts, and jewelry (unless defective)
  • Items that show signs of wear, washing, or alteration
  • Customized or personalized items

Refund Process

  • Contact us at support@thefashion.in with your order ID and reason for return
  • We will review your request within 24–48 hours
  • Once approved, you will receive return shipping instructions
  • After receiving and inspecting the returned item, your refund will be processed within 5–7 business days
  • Refunds will be credited to the original payment method

Exchanges

We currently do not offer direct exchanges. To exchange an item, please initiate a return and place a new order for the desired item.

Return Shipping

Return shipping costs are borne by the customer unless the return is due to a manufacturing defect, wrong item, or damage in transit.

Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the product. We will arrange a free pickup and provide a full refund or replacement.
